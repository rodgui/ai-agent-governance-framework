### Acceptance outcomes

- `accepted`: receiver confirms completeness and ownership;
- `conditioned`: receiver accepts with explicit conditions, owner and expiry;
- `returned`: preconditions or evidence are missing;
- `escalated`: authority or conflict exceeds receiver mandate.

Silence, elapsed time or transfer of a ticket does not equal acceptance.
