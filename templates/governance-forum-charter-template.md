## 2. Authority

The forum may:

- [ ] classify or confirm risk;
- [ ] approve;
- [ ] approve with conditions;
- [ ] hold;
- [ ] reject;
- [ ] accept residual risk within defined limits;
- [ ] grant time-bounded exceptions;
- [ ] require containment, reassessment or retirement.

The forum may **not**:

- exceed policy authority;
- treat missing evidence as approval;
- delegate accountability to an AI output;
- claim certification or independent assurance without a documented arrangement.

## 6. Decision protocol

Allowed outcomes: `approve`, `condition`, `hold`, `reject`.

Every decision records:

- subject and version;
- scope;
- rationale;
- evidence refs;
- dissent/recusal;
- conditions with owner and expiry;
- residual-risk authority;
- next review/material-change triggers;
- decision ID and system of record.
